# India

| Rule | Applies from | What the UI must do | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| RBI Responsible Business Conduct (Second Amendment) Directions, 2026 | **1 Jan 2027** (final, released 15 Jun 2026). The "July 2026" date in early press came from the Feb 2026 draft. | Ban on 11 named dark patterns (Annex IIA) for banks, NBFCs and others; separate consent per product, no common "I agree"; consent defaults to No, no pre-ticked boxes; separate opt-in for promotions and easy unsubscribe; key features (fees, interest, risks, lock-in, exit penalties) before consent; no bundling third-party products; user testing and audits; must also follow CCPA guidelines. | [consent.in summary](https://www.consent.in/blog/rbi-rbc-amendments) (secondary); RBI notification Id=13485 (not opened) |
| CCPA Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns | 30 Nov 2023 | 13 patterns: false urgency, basket sneaking, confirm shaming, forced action, subscription trap, interface interference, bait and switch, drip pricing, disguised advertisement, nagging, trick question, SaaS billing, rogue malware. Non-optional fees in the shown price; yes/no choices, not "Not now". | [AZB & Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/a-new-dawn-for-dark-patterns/) |
| RBI (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025 | 8 May 2025 | Digitally signed Key Fact Statement before signing (amount, APR, schedule, penal charges, fees, grievance officer); cooling-off ≥1 day; marketplaces show all matching offers with APR and KFS link, objective ranking only; minimal data with explicit consent, revoke and delete; grievance officer in-app. | [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/fin-tech/1636908/digital-lending-directions-2025); [RBI Id=12848](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/FS_Notification.aspx?Id=12848) |
| UPI P2P collect requests ended | 1 Oct 2025 | No person-to-person "request money" pulls on UPI; merchant collect continues. | [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/banking/story/npci-to-tighten-upi-rules-peer-to-peer-collect-feature-to-end-in-october-489244-2025-08-13) (secondary) |
| UPI Circle (delegated payments) | 2024 | Full delegation with monthly cap or partial delegation; primary user monitors and revokes any time. | [NPCI FAQ](https://npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi-circle/faqs) |
| Account Aggregator consent (Sahamati CX guidelines) | In force | First consent screen shows purpose, accounts and data types, date range, recipients, consent life, data life, fetch type and frequency, explicit grant, visible exit, trust markers, cancellation note; multilingual; data receipt after. | [Sahamati](https://sahamati.gitbook.io/customer-experience-guidelines/consent-guidelines) |

Product precedents: Google Pay India (Tez) targeted 1 GB RAM phones, 8 languages, audio-based pairing, hid phone and bank details; PhonePe's 2025 redesign put core money actions on top and the QR scanner in the thumb zone. Open design systems: CRED NeoPOP (Apache-2.0), Razorpay Blade (MIT).

Not verified: NPCI UPI PIN-screen rules, payee-name display rules, UPI Lite and 123PAY limits; Aadhaar eKYC and video KYC UX; the remaining Annex IIA pattern names.
