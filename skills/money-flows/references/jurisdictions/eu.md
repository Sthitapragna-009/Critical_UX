# European Union / EEA

| Rule | Applies from | What the UI must do | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verification of Payee (Instant Payments Regulation) | 9 Oct 2025 (euro area); 9 Jul 2027 (non-euro) | Free name check before credit transfers: match, close match (actual name shown), no match, verification not possible. Responses within seconds. | [PayTechLaw](https://paytechlaw.com/en/verification-of-payee-vop-under-the-instant-payment-regulation/); [Worldline on the EPC rulebook](https://worldline.com/en/home/main-navigation/resources/blogs/2024/navigating-the-new-verification-of-payee-rulebook) |
| European Accessibility Act | 28 Jun 2025 | Consumer banking, e-money, payment services, ATMs and terminals must be perceivable, operable, understandable, robust; information at CEFR B2 or lower; extendable time limits on terminals. | [Bird & Bird](https://cm.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/european-accessibility-act-focus-on-financial-services) |
| Consumer Credit Directive 2 (2023/2225) | 20 Nov 2026 (national laws; many states late) | Covers interest-free BNPL; "Borrowing money costs money" warning; pre-contract information; 14-day withdrawal; no consent from pre-ticked boxes for ancillary services; no unsolicited credit; human review of automated credit decisions. | [CMS](https://cms.law/en/lux/legal-updates/adoption-of-a-new-directive-modernising-credit-for-consumers); [Adyen](https://docs.adyen.com/payment-methods/bnpl-eu-regulations.md) |
| Digital Services Act Art. 25 | In force (platforms) | No interfaces that deceive or manipulate users or impair free decisions; guidance targets unequal prominence of choices, repeated asking, and cancellation harder than sign-up. | [EUR-Lex 2022/2065](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj) (wording not re-verified) |
| Digital Fairness Act | **Proposed** 11 Nov 2026; application likely years away | Would blacklist fake scarcity, confirmshaming, pre-ticked defaults; free-trial confirmation; online termination function; fines up to 6% of global turnover. | [A&O Shearman](https://www.aoshearman.com/en/insights/ao-shearman-on-data/digital-fairness-act-the-european-commissions-proposal); [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/11/11/european-commission-publishes-long-awaited-digital-fairness-act-102o9b5) |
| PSD3 / Payment Services Regulation | Provisional agreement 27 Nov 2025; application expected ~late 2027 | Name/IBAN checks for all credit transfers; spending limits and blocking tools; PSP liability for bank-impersonation fraud; access to human support; ATM and conversion fees shown before the transaction. | [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/2026/03/psd3-and-psr); [Morrison Foerster](https://www.mofo.com/pdf/resources/insights/260430-psd3-and-the-payment-services-regulation-key-developments) |
| SCA (PSD2 RTS) | In force | Exemptions: low value under €30 (until €100 or 5 transactions), TRA up to €100/€250/€500 by fraud rate, merchant-initiated. | [Stripe](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/3d-secure/strong-customer-authentication-exemptions) |
| EU Digital Identity Wallet (Reg. 2024/1183) | Wallets offered by end of 2026 | Selective disclosure, on-device data, disclosure dashboard. | [European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/digital-building-blocks/sites/display/EUDIGITALIDENTITYWALLET/EU+Digital+Identity+Wallet+Home) |
