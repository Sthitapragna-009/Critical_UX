# Singapore and Southeast Asia

Singapore has the most prescriptive anti-scam UI rules researched for this repo.

| Rule | Applies from | What the UI must do | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| MAS/ABS digital banking measures | Jan 2022 | No clickable links in SMS or email to retail customers; default transfer alert threshold S$100 or lower; at least 12 h before a new soft token works; notify the old number/email on contact changes; cooling-off before key account changes. | [MAS](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2022/mas-and-abs-announce-measures-to-bolster-the-security-of-digital-banking) |
| Shared Responsibility Framework | 16 Dec 2024 | 12 h block on high-risk actions (add payee, change contact details) after token activation or new-device login; real-time alerts for those and all outgoing payments; 24/7 reporting and a self-service kill switch. | [A&O Shearman](https://www.aoshearman.com/en/insights/ao-shearman-on-fintech-and-digital-assets/combatting-payment-account-fraud-singapores-shared-responsibility-framework) (secondary) |
| Rapid-drain holds | 16 Jun 2025 | Detect rapid account drains and hold until the customer verifies. | A&O Shearman (secondary) |
| E-Payments User Protection Guidelines | In force | Extra confirmation and tailored risk warnings before high-risk activities. | A&O Shearman (secondary) |
| Money Lock (bank practice) | 2024– | Customers can lock funds against digital transfers; reported to protect S$3.2bn across 38,000 accounts. | [Fintech News SG](https://fintechnews.sg/83258/security/singapores-money-lock-safeguards-s3-2-billion-across-38000-bank-accounts/) (headline) |

Not researched: SGQR and PayNow (Singapore), QRIS (Indonesia), PromptPay (Thailand), DuitNow (Malaysia) and GCash (Philippines) UX rules.
