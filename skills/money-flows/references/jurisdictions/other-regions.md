# Other regions and open gaps

| Market | Rule | UI implication | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| China | Payments to static merchant QR codes capped at 500 yuan per consumer per day (PBOC barcode payment specification, 2017/2018) because printed codes can be swapped | Show payee name from the rail after scanning; enter amount with payee visible | [NFC Times](https://nfctimes.com/news/china-cracks-down-static-qr-codes-regulations-take-effect-limit-risk) (secondary) |
| China, Japan, Taiwan | Red means rising and green falling in market data | Resolve gain/loss colour by market (money-data) | [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/general/education/21/10/23258001/did-you-know-you-dont-want-to-be-in-the-green-if-youre-trading-in-china-japan-or-taiwan) |
| Saudi Arabia | SAMA (2022): daily e-transfer limits; international transfers held 24 h | Show holds with the time they lift | [Asharq Al-Awsat](https://english.aawsat.com/node/3581946) (secondary) |
| Nigeria | CBN APP-fraud guidelines (**draft**, Nov 2025): 24/7 multilingual reporting channels; acknowledge within 24 h with a case number; investigate in 14 working days; reimburse within 48 h of conclusion | Report-a-scam entry and case status | [CBN PDF](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2025/CCD/Exposure%20draft%20of%20the%20Guidelines%20for%20Handling%20Authorised%20Push%20Payment%20Fraud.pdf) |
| Kenya and USSD markets | M-Pesa *334# summary-and-confirm before money moves | Read back before PIN (channels) | [Dignited](https://www.dignited.com/69230/safaricom-m-pesa-334-ussd) |
