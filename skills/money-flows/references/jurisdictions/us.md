| Reg Z §1026.60 (card applications) | In force | G-10 model table; APRs and listed fees in bold; purchase APR at least 16-point type. | [LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/12/1026.60) |

| FDIC Part 328 digital signage | Compliance **1 Apr 2027** (final rule 29 Jan 2026) | FDIC sign on the homepage/first app screen and first deposit-opening screen; non-deposit notices; dismissible notice (user or ≥3 s auto) when leaving for non-deposit platforms. | [DWT](https://www.dwt.com/blogs/financial-services-law-advisor/2026/02/fdic-finalizes-digital-signage-advertising-rule); [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/01/29/2026-01806/fdic-official-signs-advertisement-of-membership-false-advertising-misrepresentation-of-insured) |
