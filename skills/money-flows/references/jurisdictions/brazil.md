# Brazil

Most figures here come from a bank blog and press, not Banco Central texts. Verify before shipping.

| Rule | Applies from | What the UI must do | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pix night-time limit | In force | Default R$1,000 between 20h and 6h; user may move the start to 22h. Settings screen with the window and limit. | [Santander](https://www.santander.com.br/blog/novas-regras-do-pix) (secondary) |
| Limit increase delay | In force | Increases and schedule changes take 24 h; show a pending state with the time it applies. | Santander (secondary) |
| New-device limits | In force | Reduced limits for at least 24 h on a new device; explain the reason and when it lifts. | Santander (secondary) |
| Pix Automático | Jun 2025 | One prior authorisation; a management area listing mandates, with per-payment caps, suspend and cancel. | Santander; [BCB guide (PDF)](https://cdn-www.bcb.gov.br/content/estabilidadefinanceira/pix/automatico/guia_pix_automatico.pdf) (not read) |
| Pix by NFC (via Google Pay) | Feb 2025 | Default limit R$500; biometrics or passcode still required. | Santander (secondary) |
| Precautionary block | In force | Receiving bank may hold a suspicious incoming Pix up to 72 h; tell the recipient why and until when. | Santander (secondary) |
| MED dispute button | Oct 2025 | Self-service in-app dispute for fraud, scams and coercion, inside the Pix transaction. | Press headlines (details unverified) |
| Open Finance Brasil experience guide v3.03.02 | In force | Mandated journey screens for data-sharing consent and payment initiation. | [OFB guide (PDF)](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/download/attachments/17378535/GuiaDeExperienciaDoUsuarioCompartilhamentoDeDadosEIniciacaoDePagamento_v3.03.02.pdf?api=v2) (not read) |

Product precedent: Nubank moved card numbers to the back after customers posted photos of new cards; its five design principles are Start early, Co-create, Go beyond, Context, Humanize.
