# Payment and fintech case studies

What famous products did, with sources, and the rule worth keeping. Outcome numbers are rare and mostly company-reported; they're marked as such.

## Consumer payments and banking

| Product | What they did | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Wise** | True cost = fees + exchange rate; mid-market rate "you can check on Google"; each fee its own line; the same four-line quote on marketing, in-app, mid-flow and confirmation; 2024 system: "Stand apart", "Clarity for everyone", "Local at global scale", Inter for 146 languages, colours tested with WCAG and APCA ([Wise pricing](https://www.wise.com/pricing); [Wise Design](https://medium.com/transferwise-design/going-everywhere-meet-the-new-wise-design-system-863731563f71)). Transfer API maps internal states to plain stages and says "sent" isn't "arrived" ([Wise API](https://docs.wise.com/guides/send-money/tracking)) | Itemise cost before commitment; honest progress; design for every script |
| **Monzo** | Public tone guide (wit zero in ops and support) and a writing system released in Figma (2025); "magic moments" used sparingly; good friction on borrowing; Call Status banner; gambling block; disputes redesign that halved reports (company-reported, with control) ([Monzo tone](https://monzo.com/tone-of-voice/); [disputes](https://monzo.com/blog/more-than-money-back-reimagining-payment-disputes-at-monzo)); research by everyone, A/B tests with holdouts and guardrails | Predictability first, delight second; context before forms; measure with guardrails |
| **Apple Card / Wallet / Apple Pay** | Numberless titanium card, number in Wallet; ML-cleaned merchant names and colour-coded categories; real-time interest cost for each payment amount; "no late fees" headline with an interest footnote ([Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2019/03/introducing-apple-card-a-new-kind-of-credit-card-created-by-apple)). Apple Pay HIG: system sheet owns progress; business name matches the statement ([HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/apple-pay)) | Hide identifiers by default; show the cost of each choice; one merchant name everywhere |
| **Google Pay India (Tez)** | Built to "compete with cash"; audio-based pairing (80–90% accuracy) because many cameras were poor; 1 GB RAM phones; 8 languages; hid sender's phone and bank details; tested with audio-only users ([Increment](https://increment.com/frontend/case-study-mobile-payments-in-india/)). Friday scratch cards are the same mechanic regulators cited against Robinhood | Design for the cash habit and the weakest phone; protect identifiers |
| **PhonePe** (2025 redesign) | 61 crore+ users, 800+ screens; "familiarity leads to confidence"; send, self-transfer and balance kept at the top; QR scanner moved to the thumb zone "because scanning often happens under urgency"; any label change that could cause a wrong-recipient error was off-limits; release notes, interstitials and onboarding animation as scaffolding (company-reported, no outcome metrics) ([PhonePe Design](https://design.phonepe.com/stories/behind-phonepe-redesign)) | Never move money controls without transition help |
| **Cash App** | Born from "send money as easy as sending an email"; amount first; aimed at people without other banking options ([Sequoia](https://sequoiacap.com/podcast/crucible-moments-block)) | Amount-first P2P; design for the underserved |
| **Venmo** | Public-by-default payments and friends lists; global feed dropped in 2021 under privacy pressure ([EFF](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2021/07/venmo-takes-another-step-toward-privacy)) | Private by default; sharing is opt-in |
| **Revolut** | Revolut 10 (2023): "all your money" on one home, one-tap account switching, widgets, themes, after research asked to "make it easier to use" (no metrics) ([Revolut](https://www.revolut.com/blog/post/revolut-10/)) | Consolidate first, personalise second |
| **Nubank** | Five principles: Start early, Co-create, Go beyond, Context, Humanize ([Nubank](https://building.nu.com/design-principles-at-nubank/)); temporary card block before cancelling; card numbers moved to the back in 2018 after customers posted photos ([Nubank Design](https://medium.com/nubank-design/building-our-new-purple-card-669726f48db1)); 2026 rebrand differentiates through type, motion and illustration because "purple is everywhere in fintech" ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91631436/purple-is-everywhere-in-fintech-nu-bank-redesign)) | Reversible protection first; card layout is a fraud surface |
| **Klarna** | "Smoooth" brand (2017–19) and three choices (Pay now, Pay later, Slice it); later CFPB scrutiny of BNPL dark patterns; AI assistant took two-thirds of chats in its first month (vendor launch snapshot) | Credit must never look like the default |
| **Robinhood** | Commission-free simplicity; confetti, scratch-off reveals, popularity lists; confetti removed Mar 2021; $7.5M Massachusetts settlement Jan 2024 ([V&E](https://velaw.com/insights/game-over-robinhood-pays-7-5-million-to-resolve-gamification-securities-violations)); line vs candles as a setting, baseline at previous close ([support](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/using-candlestick-charts)) | No reward mechanics on risk; state every baseline |
| **M-Pesa** | USSD *334# for feature phones over 2G; summary and confirm before money moves ([Dignited](https://www.dignited.com/69230/safaricom-m-pesa-334-ussd)) | Design for the weakest channel; read back first |
| **CRED** | Premium "NeoPOP" visual language, open-sourced under Apache-2.0 for web, Android, iOS and Flutter (2022) ([GitHub](https://github.com/CRED-CLUB/neopop-web)) | A premium look can be a public system |
| **Bank of America Erica** | 3B+ interactions, ~50M users; curated library of 700+ responses; proactive insights; human handoff ([Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/artificial-intelligence/decade-ai-innovation-bofas-virtual-assistant-erica-surpasses-3-billion)) | Bounded assistants with handoff |

## Business and infrastructure

| Product | What they did | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Stripe** | Payment Element orders methods by device and locale; Appearance API themes tokens, not structure; ~25 roles separating access from money; decline codes mapped to actions; docs as product ([Stripe](https://stripe.com/blog/introducing-the-payment-element); [roles](https://docs.stripe.com/get-started/account/teams/roles)). Conversion uplifts (11.9% revenue, 34% at Italic) are company-reported without methodology | Localise methods; bounded theming; permissions as design |
| **Square** | Separate reader so customers tap their own card ("my space and your space"); Register with a customer display and offline mode ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/3057852/the-design-challenge-behind-squares-new-reader)) | Two audiences at the counter; offline is a feature |
| **Plaid** | Host explains why, benefit, security and what you'll need before Link; Plaid owns consent ([Plaid](https://plaid.com/docs/link/best-practices/)) | The host owns the frame in embedded flows |
| **Mercury** | Separation-of-duties toggle: creator can't approve own payment; blocks if no other approver ([Mercury](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/45985054591508-Enforcing-separation-of-duties-for-per-payment-approvals)) | Four-eyes as a product setting |
| **Ramp** | Approvals routed by amount; request and approve in Slack; "speed" as a company principle ([Ramp](https://ramp.com/blog/multistep-approvals-for-corporate-cards-reimbursements)) | Meet approvers where they work |
| **Coinbase** | Open CDS with a documented chart Scrubber (Apache-2.0) ([CDS](https://cds.coinbase.com/components/charts/Scrubber/)) | Specify chart interactions, then add keyboard access |
| **Razorpay** | Open Blade design system (MIT) ([GitHub](https://github.com/razorpay/blade)) | |

Not researched yet: Paytm, Jupiter, Fi, Mercado Pago, PicPay, GCash, Alipay and WeChat Pay mini-programs, Chime, Brex, Starling, N26, Zerodha, Groww.
